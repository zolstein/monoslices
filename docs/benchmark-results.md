# Benchmark Results

These benchmark results compare operations generated by `monoslices` and their analogous `slices.*Func` operations, measured by the benchmarks in `internal/benchmarks`. Percentage changes are differences in `sec/op` reported by `benchstat` across six samples. Negative percentages mean `monoslices` is faster.

Benchmarks were compiled with Go 1.27.1, for linux/amd64 with `GOAMD64=v3`.
